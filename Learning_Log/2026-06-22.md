## Connections made

- Forecasting predicts operational demand; experimentation estimates whether a product change causes improvement.
- Uplift modeling connects causal effects to personalization by estimating heterogeneous treatment effects.
- CUPED and sequential testing improve experimental efficiency or decision timing but do not replace sound randomization and measurement.
- Offline model validation and online causal impact answer different questions.

## One worked example

For an AI assistant used by customer-support experts: forecast contact demand, rank answer suggestions with a recommendation model, estimate product impact with an A/B test, improve precision with CUPED, estimate heterogeneous benefit with uplift modeling, and monitor accuracy, safety, latency, repeat contacts, and escalations in production.
