# DS interview learning roadmap

## Phase 1 — Statistical foundations

- Estimands, samples, and sampling distributions
- Confidence intervals and hypothesis tests
- Type I/II error, power, MDE, and practical significance
- Common tests for means, proportions, and categorical outcomes

## Phase 2 — Experimentation

- Randomization unit, balance, exposure, duration, and interference
- Primary metrics and guardrails
- Superiority and non-inferiority
- Multiple testing, novelty, peeking, and sequential decisions
- ITT, adoption, noncompliance, and delayed outcomes

## Phase 3 — Product and risk cases

- Metric trees and segmentation
- Funnel and cohort analysis
- Fraud/risk trade-offs and false positives
- Rollout, monitoring, operational capacity, and decision rules

## Phase 4 — Causal inference

- Potential outcomes, DAGs, and identification
- Difference-in-differences
- Regression discontinuity
- Matching and weighting
- Instrumental variables
- Synthetic controls

## Phase 5 — Predictive modeling

- Linear and logistic regression
- Trees, random forests, and gradient boosting
- Validation, regularization, leakage, and class imbalance
- Ranking, thresholds, calibration, and monitoring

## Phase 6 — Role-specific extensions

- CUPED and other variance-reduction methods
- Uplift modeling
- Bayesian methods
- Multi-armed bandits
- AI/LLM evaluation and safe orchestration

Do not advance based on exposure alone. Use worked problems and retrieval quizzes to demonstrate mastery.
